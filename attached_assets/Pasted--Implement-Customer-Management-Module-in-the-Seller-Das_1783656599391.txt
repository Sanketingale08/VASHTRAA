# Implement Customer Management Module in the Seller Dashboard

Implement a complete **Customer Management** module in the **Seller Dashboard**. This module should allow sellers to manage customers who have purchased products from **their own shop**.

Only the logged-in seller should be able to access this module and view customer information related to their own orders.

## Features to Implement

### Customer Details

Allow sellers to view:

* Customer Name
* Customer ID
* Email Address
* Phone Number
* Shipping Address
* Billing Address
* Total Orders
* Total Amount Spent
* Last Order Date
* Customer Status (Active/Inactive)

> **Note:** Sellers should only be able to view customers who have placed orders from their own shop.

---

### Customer Purchase History

Allow sellers to view:

* Complete Order History
* Purchased Products
* Order Dates
* Order Status
* Payment Status
* Total Purchase Amount
* Invoice Details

Provide filters by:

* Date Range
* Order Status
* Payment Status

---

### Customer Reviews

Allow sellers to:

* View Product Reviews
* View Customer Ratings
* Reply to Customer Reviews
* Report Inappropriate Reviews
* Filter Reviews by Rating and Date

Display:

* Product Name
* Customer Name
* Rating
* Review Comment
* Review Date
* Seller Reply

---

### Customer Queries

Allow sellers to:

* View Customer Questions
* Reply to Customer Queries
* Mark Queries as Resolved
* Track Query Status
* View Conversation History

Display:

* Customer Name
* Product Name
* Query Subject
* Message
* Date & Time
* Status (Open, In Progress, Resolved)

---

### Customer Support Tickets

Allow sellers to:

* View Support Tickets
* Reply to Customer Tickets
* Update Ticket Status
* Assign Priority
* Close Resolved Tickets
* View Ticket History

Support ticket statuses should include:

* Open
* In Progress
* Waiting for Customer
* Resolved
* Closed

Priority levels:

* Low
* Medium
* High
* Urgent

---

## Customer Management Dashboard

Display summary cards for:

* Total Customers
* New Customers
* Repeat Customers
* Customer Reviews
* Open Queries
* Open Support Tickets
* Average Customer Rating

---

## Real-Time Synchronization

All customer-related activities should automatically synchronize with the **Customer Portal** and **Super Admin Panel**.

When a customer:

* Places an Order
* Submits a Review
* Creates a Support Ticket
* Sends a Product Query

The corresponding seller should receive a real-time notification, and the data should immediately appear in the Seller Dashboard.

---

## Seller Permissions

Each seller should only be able to:

* View customers who purchased from their own shop.
* View purchase history for their own products.
* Reply to reviews and customer queries related to their own products.
* Manage support tickets related to their own customers.

A seller must **not** be able to access customer information, orders, reviews, queries, or support tickets belonging to another seller.

---

## Additional Requirements

* Build the Customer Management module with a premium, modern, and fully responsive UI.
* Include advanced search, filtering, sorting, and pagination.
* Display customer statistics using summary cards and charts where applicable.
* Secure all customer data with role-based access control.
* Preserve all existing functionality and ensure this implementation does not affect any existing routing, APIs, database relationships, or business logic.
