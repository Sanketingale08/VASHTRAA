# Implement Product & Inventory Management in the Seller Dashboard

Implement a complete **Product Management** and **Inventory Management** module within the **Seller Dashboard**.

Only the logged-in seller should be able to manage their own products and inventory.

---

## Product Management

Allow sellers to create and manage products with the following details:

### Add Product

The **Add Product** form should include:

* Product Name
* Category
* Subcategory
* Brand Name
* Product Description
* Fabric Details
* Color
* Design Pattern
* Price
* Discount
* Stock Quantity
* Multiple Product Images
* Product Videos
* Product Variants (Size, Color, etc., if applicable)
* Product Specifications
* Delivery Information
* Return Policy

All fields should be validated before the product is published.

---

## Product Actions

Allow sellers to perform the following actions:

* Add Products
* Edit Products
* Delete Products
* Activate Products
* Deactivate Products
* Duplicate Products
* Bulk Product Upload (CSV/Excel)

Display all products in a modern, searchable, and filterable data table with actions for viewing, editing, deleting, activating, and deactivating products.

---

## Inventory Management

Implement a complete inventory management system with the following features:

* Available Stock
* Low Stock Alerts
* Out of Stock Alerts
* Stock Update History
* Bulk Stock Updates
* Inventory Reports
* Bulk Price Updates

Inventory should automatically update whenever:

* A customer places an order
* An order is cancelled
* A return is completed
* The seller manually updates stock

---

## Product Visibility

Products added by a seller should automatically appear in the **Customer Portal** after meeting the marketplace's approval workflow (or immediately if product approval is disabled).

Whenever a seller:

* Adds a product
* Updates product information
* Changes the price
* Applies a discount
* Updates stock quantity
* Activates or deactivates a product

The changes should automatically synchronize with the Customer Portal and the Super Admin Panel in real time.

---

## Seller Permissions

Each seller should only be able to:

* View their own products
* Add products to their own shop
* Edit their own products
* Delete their own products
* Manage their own inventory

Sellers must **not** be able to access or modify products belonging to other sellers.

---

## Additional Requirements

* Build the Product Management and Inventory Management modules with a premium, modern, and responsive UI.
* Include advanced search, filtering, sorting, and pagination for product listings.
* Support image and video uploads with previews.
* Validate all required fields before saving products.
* Ensure all APIs, database operations, and routing are secure and role-based.
* Preserve all existing functionality and ensure this implementation does not affect any existing features, routing, APIs, database relationships, or business logic.
