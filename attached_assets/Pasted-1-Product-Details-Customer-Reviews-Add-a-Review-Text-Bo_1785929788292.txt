Change Email Address Workflow

•	Open a secure modal or dedicated page.
•	Prompt the user to enter a new email address.
•	Validate the email format.
•	Ensure the new email address is not already registered.

•	Display an OTP/verification screen.
•	Allow OTP resend with a countdown timer.

•	Implement rate limiting to prevent OTP abuse.
 
•	Allow OTP resend only after the countdown timer expires.

•	Encrypt all sensitive data during transmission.
